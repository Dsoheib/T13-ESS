## Data availability
The instrument, simulation, diagnostics, screening log, and verbatim criteria are released as a
reproducible artifact archived from git tag `submission-v2`; `make all` regenerates every table.
Public benchmarks are cited, not redistributed (CICIDS2017: Sharafaldin et al., 2018). The AAL
MQTT-bridge window features underlying the window-grain results are available from the
corresponding author on reasonable request; the raw MQTT alert stream is not redistributed, as it
was captured from a live ambient-assisted-living deployment.
